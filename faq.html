Ask us in a demo

General

Is this only a web app?

No. OneKinder includes role-based web portals and mobile apps for parents, teachers and administrators.

Can it manage multiple branches?

Yes. Schools can be grouped into branches with separate settings, branch-level admins and scoped access for each leader.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on your setup. We look at the number of schools, branches and users before sharing a quote. See our pricing page.

Access & roles

What does each role see?

Platform administrators manage schools and branding, superadmins manage schools or groups, branch admins run daily operations, teachers manage classes and parents see their own children.

Is two-factor authentication available?

Yes. TOTP-based two-factor authentication can be set up with a QR code and enabled for accounts that need extra protection.

Can I try it without touching real data?

Yes. Demo accounts let people explore and create sample records but cannot edit or delete existing records or change credentials.

Attendance & fees

Is teacher attendance location-verified?

Yes. Check-in and check-out capture coordinates and accuracy, with a configurable geofence radius and rejection of inaccurate readings.

Can parents pay fees online?

Yes. Razorpay is integrated for fee and admission payments. Each school can enable or disable online payments.

Does it support offline payments?

Yes. Cash, UPI, cheque and demand draft collections can be recorded and tracked alongside online transactions.

Admissions & data

Can schools track admissions?

Yes. There is a public application flow, document upload, status tracking and an admin back office that converts applicants into students.

Can we customise branding?

Yes. The SaaS logo and title can be changed centrally, and each school can set its own identity and login branding.

What if we delete something by mistake?

Students, teachers and classrooms use soft deletion, so items can be restored from the trash before permanent deletion.
