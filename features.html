Core modules

One system, no gaps between teams.

Every module shares the same students, classes and settings — so nothing has to be typed twice.

Attendance & geofencing

Bulk student attendance, holidays, configurable late and half-day cutoffs, and GPS-verified teacher check-in with accuracy checks.

Fees & payments

Bulk invoices, Razorpay orders and verification, offline cash/UPI/cheque/DD records, receipts and automatic overdue reminders.

Admissions

Public application form, document uploads, status workflow, admission invoices and one-click student record generation.

Students & classrooms

Student CRUD, roll numbers, parent links, bulk CSV/XLSX import, promotion, graduation, transfer and trash/restore.

Communication

Targeted announcements, homework with attachments, photo activities, diary entries, messaging and read receipts.

Reports & analytics

KPI cards and ApexCharts for enrolment, attendance, fees, staff and admissions, scoped to the right school group.

Multi-branch control

School groups, branch admins, inherited settings, safe deletion rules, impersonation and group-level oversight.

Branding & white label

Editable SaaS title and logo, school identity, branded login, custom admin display name and cached login branding.

Mobile app platform

Branded parent, teacher and admin Flutter apps, APK releases with version codes, force updates and school splash screens.

Email & push notifications

Per-school email templates, logs and test sends, plus Firebase Cloud Messaging alerts delivered in the background.

Calendar & events

Events with class targeting, teacher drafts with admin approval, tomorrow reminders and holidays that populate attendance.

Settings & demo mode

Attendance cutoffs, geofence radius, storage backend, timezone, ID formats and a safe demo mode for guided exploration.

Connected by design

Built to work together, not side by side.

Your tools, plugged into the flow

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push alerts

Roles, access and safety

Each person sees exactly what their job needs. Two-factor authentication, scoped school groups and a guarded demo mode keep data where it belongs.

  • Role-based redirects to the right dashboard
  • Optional TOTP two-factor authentication
  • Group superadmins restricted to their branches
  • Demo users cannot edit or delete real records
