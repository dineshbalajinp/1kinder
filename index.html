Questions, answered

Good to know before you start.

Can't find what you're looking for? Ask us in a demo — we'll walk through your school's setup.

Is this only a web app?

No. OneKinder includes role-based web portals and mobile apps for parents, teachers and administrators.

Can it manage multiple branches?

Yes. Schools can be grouped into branches with separate settings, branch-level admins and scoped access for each leader.

Can parents pay fees online?

Yes. Razorpay is integrated for fee and admission payments, and each school can enable or disable online payments.

Does it support offline payments?

Yes. Cash, UPI, cheque and demand draft collections can be recorded and tracked alongside online transactions.

Is teacher attendance location-verified?

Yes. Teacher check-in and check-out capture GPS coordinates and accuracy, with a configurable geofence radius and cutoff rules.

Is there a safe way to explore the product first?

Yes. Demo accounts let new users explore the portals and create sample records without changing real school data.
