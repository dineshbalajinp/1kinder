Privacy Policy

How information is handled when a school uses OneKinder.

Draft notice: This is a template for review, not legal advice. Replace it with a policy approved for your business and jurisdiction before launch. Last updated: 23 November 2026.

1. Who we are

OneKinder is a preschool and kindergarten management platform provided to schools, teachers, parents and applicants. The school that invites you to use OneKinder is responsible for its own records and for the accuracy of the information it enters.

2. Information we handle

Depending on your role, OneKinder may process account details such as names, email addresses and phone numbers; student and classroom records; attendance and fee information; documents uploaded for admissions; messages, homework, diary and activity content; and device tokens used for push notifications.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide the platform: routing users to the right dashboard, keeping school records, processing fees and admissions, sending announcements and reminders, and producing reports for school leaders. Children's information is used for school administration and parent communication only.

4. Service providers

OneKinder integrates with third-party services chosen or configured by the school, which may include Razorpay for payments, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, SMTP email providers, and Cloudinary, Google Drive, Cloudflare R2 or local storage for files. Each provider processes information according to its own terms.

5. Access and roles

Access is limited by role. Platform administrators, group superadmins, branch admins, teachers and parents see different views of information. Optional two-factor authentication is available for added account protection.

6. Retention and deletion

Schools control their records. Some records use soft deletion so they can be restored from a trash area before permanent deletion. Retention periods should be set by each school to meet its local requirements.

7. Your choices

If you are a parent or teacher and believe your information is incorrect, contact your school. If you are a school, contact us using the details below to discuss data access, correction or removal requests.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@onekinder.example.
